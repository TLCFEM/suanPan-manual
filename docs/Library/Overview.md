This section presents the details of all available elements/sections/materials/solvers, etc.

Readers shall be able to find the corresponding syntax and theoretical background.

## At a Glance

The library is organised into the following areas.

| Area | What you get | Details |
|:-----|:-------------|:--------|
| Analysis | static, dynamic, arc-length, frequency, buckling and optimization steps | [Step overview](Step/Overview.md) |
| Elements | beams, trusses, membranes, plates, shells, 3D solids, contact, joints, mass and springs | [model library](../SUMMARY.md) |
| Materials | elasticity, plasticity, concrete damage, orthotropic, hysteresis and user-defined models | [Material overview](Material/Overview.md) |
| Sections | structural, fibre, NM and code-based design sections | [model library](../SUMMARY.md) |
| Constraints | MPC, rigid and restitution walls, embedding, node-line/facet and gap constraints | [Constraint](Constraint/Constraint.md) |
| Integrators | implicit and explicit time-integration schemes | [Integrator overview](Integrator/Overview.md) |
